THE INFIELD

Designing Regenerative Futures. Growing with Nature.
The agroecological and farm design vertical of Baradia Group, dedicated to creating sustainable, productive, and harmonious landscapes that balance nature, people, and prosperity.
Vision & Mission
We specialize in farm planning, ecological land design, and regenerative agriculture systems — combining scientific precision with a deep respect for natural processes.
Vision
To transform how the world cultivates land — building a future where every farm is profitable, ecological, and resilient.
Mission
To integrate science, design, and sustainability into farming systems that respect ecology, empower farmers, and regenerate the Earth — one landscape at a time.
Our Philosophy
We believe the field is our classroom, our laboratory, and our future. At The InField, design begins with observation and empathy — understanding the land's rhythm, the farmer's need, and the ecosystem's potential. Our goal is not to fight nature, but to work with it intelligently and gracefully.
What We Do
Comprehensive agroecological solutions for sustainable land management and farming.
  • Agroecological Farm Design
    Comprehensive farm master planning based on soil, water, climate, crop patterns, and livelihood goals.
  • Permaculture & Regenerative Agriculture Systems
    Designing food forests, polyculture systems, and circular farm economies that enrich ecosystems and communities.
  • Water & Soil Management
    Innovative solutions for water harvesting, open wells, drainage design, soil fertility building, and carbon farming.
  • Sustainable Infrastructure & Layout Planning
    Farmhouses, roads, energy systems, and storage planned with functionality, aesthetics, and environmental sensitivity.
  • Training & Advisory
    Empowering farmers, entrepreneurs, and institutions through field workshops and capacity-building programs.

"We are not just consultants — we are stewards of the land."
